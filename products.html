Discovery sources used for this initial landscape

These sources were used to discover categories, products, and providers—not as endorsements or as evidence that a vendor satisfies a standard.

  • OECD Catalogue of Tools & Metrics for Trustworthy AI — lifecycle, trustworthiness, auditing, and measurement concepts.
  • NIST AI Resource Center — AI RMF operationalization and testing, evaluation, verification, and validation resources.
  • Ethical AI Database market map — responsible-AI company and product discovery.
  • AI Security Startups Map — AI and agent-security vendor discovery and category inspiration.

How to read this matrix

  • Inclusion is not an endorsement. Products have not been independently tested for quality, effectiveness, or fitness for a particular use.
  • Standards relevance is editorial. It identifies potentially useful standards or frameworks to investigate; it is not a vendor certification or compliance determination.
  • Provider types are intentionally broad. The landscape includes startups, established platforms, integrated acquisitions, open-source projects, and assurance organizations.
  • Physical-AI relevance is editorial, using the same High, Medium, and Low convention as the standards matrix.

Coverage and verification

This is a curated starting point, not an exhaustive market catalogue or procurement recommendation. Offerings, ownership, availability, and vendor claims change quickly. Follow the provider link and obtain current technical, contractual, security, and assurance evidence before relying on an entry.
