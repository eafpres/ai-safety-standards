How to read this matrix

  • Standards are not interchangeable. Management-system, product-safety, functional-safety, security, and regulatory publications solve different parts of the assurance problem.
  • “Published” does not mean “certifiable.” The conformity column identifies management-system certification, product certification, type approval, regulatory review, or supporting-only use.
  • EU presumption of conformity is specific. It normally arises only when a harmonised standard is cited in the Official Journal for the relevant legislation.
  • Physical-AI relevance is an editorial classification, not a designation made by the issuing body.

Coverage and access

This is a curated decision-support map, not an exhaustive catalogue or legal opinion. Each publication link opens the official record for that exact deliverable. “Free full text” means the official issuer provides the complete document without payment; “Abstract free; full text paid” means the record and limited preview are public. Drafts and work items can change; re-check the linked source before using a status in a compliance plan.
